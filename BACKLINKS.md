Quality > quantity — 5 strong listings beat 50 spammy ones.

  and suggest it as an alternative to similar dev-tool sites. https://alternativeto.net/

## Notes

- Backlinks take weeks–months to move rankings. This is slow-burn, not instant.
- Nofollow links (like AlternativeTo's) still drive traffic and brand signals — worth it.
- Never buy backlinks — Google penalizes link schemes; these organic listings are safe.
